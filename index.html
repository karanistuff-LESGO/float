Flowers

Flowers are the reproductive structures of angiosperms (flowering plants). They are often brightly colored and fragrant to attract pollinators such as bees, butterflies, and birds. Flowers come in a wide variety of shapes, sizes, and colors, and they play a crucial role in the life cycle of plants by facilitating pollination and reproduction.
